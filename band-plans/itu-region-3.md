does not include mode or license-class breakdowns — just band edges and
band names. Individual Region 3 countries impose their own mode/license-
class sub-band rules within these edges, which are not captured here.

## Known data-quality caveats

This region's data has **not** been cross-checked against an
authoritative source (e.g. IARU Region 3's own published band plan) the
way Region 2's table was against 47 CFR 97.301. No 4m or 1.25m entries
exist in the source for this region (unlike Region 1's 4m or Region 2's
1.25m) — this is reproduced as-is, not a known omission to fill in.
