## Common PSK31 Frequencies

PSK31 activity traditionally concentrates from the bottom edge of the
IARU RTTY band plan upward as activity increases (10m is an exception,
defined 150 Hz above that edge to accommodate hams without full
privileges). Only ~100 Hz of channel separation is really needed.
These are IARU Region 1 recommended frequencies — Regions 2 and 3 may
differ (noted below for 40m).

1838.150, 3580.150, 7035.15 (Region 1 & 3) / 7080.15 (Region 2 — wider
40m allocation), 10142.150, 14070.150, 18100.150, 21080.150 (though
most activity is found ~10 kHz lower), 24920.150, 28120.150 kHz.
